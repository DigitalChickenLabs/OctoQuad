**Field-Upgradable Firmware

1 Firmware files

From time to time, official firmware updates for the OctoQuad may be released. 
Official firmware binaries are found here. 

WARNING: Flashing unofficial firmware may cause permanent damage to the OctoQuad, or to devices to which it is
connected.

2 Flashing firmware

To flash a firmware image onto the OctoQuad, follow the procedure below:

a. Remove all power and data connections from the OctoQuad.
b. Press and hold the BOOTSEL button (‘B’ on resin printed case)
c. While holding BOOTSEL, connect the OctoQuad to a computer using the micro-USB port
d. Wait until the emulated USB drive appears on the computer. The LED will remain off.
e. Drag-n-drop the firmware image onto the emulated USB drive
f. The OctoQuad will automatically flash the firmware and reboot. Flashing is complete
when the emulated USB drive disappears and the status LED begins blinking an interface
code.
